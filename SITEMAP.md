# Sitemap

The index.html (home page) should be modeled after Teddy's home page (https://theodore.net/), in particular the following:
- Simple white background and black or dark gray text.
- The home icon in the top left as a fairly sized graphic sketch of my face. However, I want mine to be a bit dynamic, in that when i hover over it, it switches to a version that is identical except I'm smiling. So, two versions of the icon. One for when someone's hovering over it and one when someone's not. And when you click it, it goes back to index.html (home).
- Said icon shrinks a little on scrolling, but remains at 100% and as an overlay of the below content. Meanwhile, the whole horizontal header has a slightly blurred/translucent overlay over the content for the top inch or so.
- Toggle button in top right for light/night mode.

However, some differences:
- I don't need a search bar.
- I don't want the hamburger style collapsed menu in top right, at least for desktop. I want a static menu bar in the header that's fairly small, clean, condensed, centered, for five pages: About, Gallery, EPILOGUE, OFF-SYLLABUS, and Store.
- The whole page should be relatively [minimal/clean — as discussed]

All of the above should apply to all pages of my site.

Ok, in terms of the content of the index.html page. I like the simple, larger "Hey! I'm Teddy, a __" that dynamically types and switches out. I want to keep that. And, the sentence beneath, I think I'll probably have 3 sentences there.

Then, beneath that initial bio, I want the content to be tripartite (in three columns), in the form of three simple (thin black outline) boxes, with curved corners:
- On the left, a "Things I Believe" box with simple bullet point list of things I believe. Like nat.org has on the homepage.
- In the middle, a "Lately" box with some bullet points of my current projects and open questions. Also maybe three simple goals each accompanied by a simple horizontal progress bar graphic that shows the level of progress toward that goal (the % of which I can set in the code, or maybe later can be tied to some API).
- On the right, "Next" box with what I'm looking forward to and what's coming up! Maybe 2–3 countdowns (X days until…) to big days that I set.

And that should be it for the home page! Teddy's footer is nice as a start as well.

In addition to the home page, there should be five additional pages directly accessible via the ever-present menu bar, in this order:

## 1. About (davidkhays.com/about)
Toggle slider at the top to switch between three contents:
- **Interactive CV** — a vertical page that resembles a CV but has annotations and links and images when you hover over things.
- **Obituary** — simpler. Just a few images and mostly a big block of simple text.
- **Library** — a 'favorite things' section modeled after other sites, a collapsible list (https://www.sunkht.xyz/favorites/). Preliminary structure:
  - Reading: Quotes, Poems, Articles, Papers, Books
  - Watching: Short-Form, YouTube, TV Shows, Movies
  - Looking: Art, Architecture
  - Listening: Songs, Concerts
  - Tasting: Recipes, Restaurants
  - Playing: Board Games, Video Games
  - Visiting: Places, Bucket List

## 2. Gallery (davidkhays.com/gallery)
Essentially a long blog post, with three main sections as bookmarks hyperlinked at the top so someone can skip: 1. Tokyo (2004–2025), 2. Hawaii (2015–2022), 3. My Oxford Years (2022–2026). Interwoven with text, a lot of carousels containing photos navigable horizontally like a gallery.

## 3. EPILOGUE (davidkhays.com/epilogue)
A writings section (essentially a blog). Landing should be a list, most recent at top — model exactly after https://theodore.net/writings/.

## 4. OFF-SYLLABUS (davidkhays.com/off-syllabus)
Home of curricula I'm writing. Starting with just Neuroscience — landing is a simple box with a course description; clicking it goes to davidkhays.com/off-syllabus/neuro, another writings-style section like theodore.net/writings but separate from EPILOGUE. Each post is a lesson, so not recent-first — sequential and modular, probably a grid rather than a list.

## 5. Store (davidkhays.com/store)
Placeholder landing for future merchandise — maybe just collect email for now.
