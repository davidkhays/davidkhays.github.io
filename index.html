Hi! I'm David, a

I spent my formative years in Tokyo, navigated adolescence in Hawai'i, then moved to the UK to study the brain and behavior. I'm interested in all aspects of the human condition, but especially the neurobiology of reward and how it can be usurped — by disease and by design.

I built this site so you can learn more about me and check out some of my photos. I'm also trying to create stuff more consistently (and conscientiously!) here, and I'm working on developing an accessible, foundation-level neuroscience curriculum, soon to be here.
